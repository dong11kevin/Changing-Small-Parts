Welcome to L-System Morphogenesis

QUICK START

This interactive playground lets you watch simple genetic text strings grow into complex digital organisms using biological growth rules!

Step 1: Choose a Mode. Use the tab buttons at the top of the left sidebar to switch between the Intro guide and four distinct experimental modes (Letter, String, Phrase, or Testing).

Step 2: Tweak the Rules. Modify starting seed strings and replacement rules, then use the growth turns slider to watch your digital creature evolve shapes, colors, and armor spikes in real-time.

Step 3: Experiment Freely. Test out different configurations, or use the Smart Recipe Finder in Testing mode to automatically calculate growth codes matching your desired physical features[cite: 1].
